Cleaning should match how the premises are used.

Restaurant cleaning depends on timing, hygiene and a clear division of responsibilities. We agree which areas are included and how cleaning fits around kitchen and customer-service operations.

What can be included?

  • Customer areas and floors
  • Washrooms and high-touch surfaces
  • Staff and social areas
  • Agreed back-of-house areas
  • Agreed waste and recycling tasks
  • Deeper cleaning under a separate plan
The final scope is defined in the quotation and service plan. This avoids ambiguity about what is included in recurring cleaning and what is additional work.

The right frequency, not a generic package

We look beyond square metres to how the space is used, visitor volume, surfaces and hygiene needs. Cleaning may be several times per week, weekly or another agreed frequency.
