Quality and service process

A clear service description, one communication route and controlled changes.

Service model

Quality should not depend on someone remembering an unwritten detail.

Good commercial cleaning is built on a clear service description, one communication route and documented changes instead of assumptions.

1

Site review

We review the spaces, occupancy, critical areas, working hours and target cleanliness level.

2

Service description

Tasks, frequencies, boundaries, extras and access arrangements are agreed in writing.

3

One contact route

Quote, service and change requests are routed clearly through the same customer-service channel.

4

Review and change

If the use of the space, headcount or cleaning need changes, the service description can be updated.

Deviations

Observation → service request → corrective action.

Existing customers can report a quality issue or additional need through the service-request form, including the site and issue type in one message.

Send a service request →

What we do not promise without agreement

  • an automatic 24-hour correction guarantee
  • certifications the business does not hold
  • specialist work without required competence or safety arrangements
  • extra tasks that are not included in agreed pricing

Trust is better built with precise promises than exaggerated claims.
