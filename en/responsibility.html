Responsibility

Responsibility should show up in everyday choices.

Responsibility should show up in everyday choices.

We do not fill this page with certifications we do not hold. Instead, we describe what we can do in practice: use appropriate methods, avoid unnecessary consumption of chemicals and water, follow product instructions and organise work so quality comes from consistent routines.

Environment

The aim is to use chemicals, water and equipment according to need, rather than simply using more. Methods should match the surface and soil level. Microfibre, correct dosing and an efficient work sequence can reduce unnecessary consumption.

Work safety

Site access, key and safety instructions are reviewed before work begins. Tasks with special risks are assessed separately.

A fair service scope

Responsibility also means not promising work that has not been agreed or cannot be done safely. The quotation separates recurring work from additional tasks.

Our principles

  • Fit-for-purpose methods
  • Correct dosing
  • Safe working
  • Clear responsibilities
  • No unsupported environmental claims
