Maintenance cleaning or deep cleaning?

Maintenance cleaning preserves an agreed level. Deep cleaning restores surfaces more thoroughly when the routine is no longer enough.

Maintenance cleaning preserves the level

Maintenance cleaning repeats agreed tasks regularly to prevent uncontrolled build-up and keep premises ready for use.

Deep cleaning restores

Deep cleaning tackles more persistent soil, harder-to-reach areas or floors with methods that are not carried out on every visit.

The best approach is an annual plan

When routine and infrequent tasks are planned together, the customer does not have to notice every need only after it becomes visible.
