How often should an office be cleaned?

Cleaning frequency should be based on occupancy, staff numbers and room types, not square metres alone.

Cleaning frequency should be based on occupancy, staff numbers and room types, not square metres alone.

Square metres do not decide frequency on their own

Two offices of the same size can need very different cleaning input. Staff numbers, hybrid work, visitor traffic, entrances, kitchens, washrooms and flooring all affect workload.

Give different areas different rhythms

Not every room needs the same frequency. Washrooms and kitchens may require daily or several-times-weekly attention while a lightly used meeting room can be serviced less often.

Start with a scope and review it

The first schedule is a starting point, not a permanent truth. After a few weeks, review where soil builds up and where the service is over-dimensioned.
