Restaurant cleanliness and Oiva: what should a cleaning partner understand?

Oiva is official food control. A cleaning partner supports the operator’s self-monitoring through clear responsibilities and repeatable routines.

Oiva is an official control system

Oiva guidance is used by food-control authorities. Cleanliness is one part of the assessment, but a cleaning company does not issue Oiva grades and cannot guarantee a specific result.

Write responsibilities down

Production-time cleanliness, equipment interiors, degreasing, floor drains, waste areas and customer spaces may belong to different people. Unclear boundaries quickly become hygiene risks.

Self-monitoring should guide cleaning

A good cleaning partner aligns work instructions with the restaurant’s self-monitoring system and documents tasks so delivery can be followed up.
