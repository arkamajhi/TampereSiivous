# Competitor-informed improvements (V3)

Reviewed 27 Sep 2026: tampereensiivous.fi, huomenta.fi and campusta.fi.

## Patterns adopted in original form

- Separate route for existing-customer service requests.
- One-contact / one-channel communication logic.
- Interactive cleaning-needs and frequency assessment before quote request.
- Clearer quality and service-process explanation.
- Stronger distinction between new-customer quote requests and existing-customer operational requests.
- Existing add-on-service content retained and made more visible, including consumable refills and coordination of support services.
- Existing case-study framework retained: future references should explain site type, starting situation, service rhythm, solution and outcome.

## Deliberately not copied

- Competitor wording, branding, layouts or images.
- NPS/customer-satisfaction statistics.
- Corporate customer logos or named references.
- Certifications or trust badges.
- P1/P2 expertise claims.
- Fixed response-time guarantees.
- Competitor-specific prices.

These should only be added to the public site when Tampere Siivous/Tyozy has evidence and permission to substantiate them.
